# StreamFree — Licensing Provider Questionnaire

**Purpose:** a structured set of questions to ask any content-licensing provider, compiled
from the requirements established in `docs/content-provider-requirements.md` and the
open questions identified during Stage 8 research. This document does not represent that
any of these questions have been asked or answered — it is preparation only.

**Important reminder carried forward from Stage 8:** a provider confirming general
"AVOD" or "ad-supported" rights does **not** confirm that StreamFree's specific rewarded-ad-unlock
mechanic is permitted, and does not confirm web-streaming rights distinct from mobile/app
rights. These are separate questions and must each be asked and answered individually —
never inferred from one another.

---

## 1. Platform Eligibility

1.1. Does [Provider] license content to new, independent streaming platforms — not only established/existing partners?
1.2. Is there a minimum size, traffic, funding, or track-record requirement for a new platform to be considered?
1.3. What is the typical timeline from initial inquiry to a signed agreement?

## 2. Web Streaming Rights

2.1. Does the license explicitly cover **web-based** streaming (a browser-based application), as opposed to only mobile apps or connected-TV apps?
2.2. If the provider's existing distribution is primarily mobile/in-app, does that limit or exclude web delivery?

## 3. Commercial Rights

3.1. Is commercial use of the licensed content explicitly permitted?
3.2. Does the license distinguish between different commercial models (subscription, transactional, ad-supported)?

## 4. AVOD Rights

4.1. Is ad-supported (AVOD) viewing explicitly permitted?
4.2. Are there any restrictions on the format, frequency, or placement of advertising?

## 5. Third-Party Advertising

5.1. May StreamFree sell/serve its own third-party advertising around the licensed content, or does the provider require use of its own ad stack?
5.2. Are there restrictions on which ad networks/platforms may be used?

## 6. Rewarded-Advertisement Unlock

6.1. Does the license specifically permit a "watch a rewarded ad to unlock this title" access mechanic, as distinct from general pre-roll/display advertising?
6.2. Is there any restriction on how long the unlocked access may last, or how it must be re-verified?

## 7. Google Ad Manager Compatibility

7.1. Is there any restriction on which ad-serving technology (e.g., Google Ad Manager specifically) may be used alongside the licensed content?
7.2. Does the provider require disclosure or approval of the specific ad-tech stack used?

## 8. Philippines Eligibility

8.1. Is a Philippines-based company/operator eligible to license content under this program?
8.2. Does the provider have any existing relationships, restrictions, or requirements specific to the Philippines?

## 9. Southeast Asia Rights

9.1. Is Southeast Asia (as a region) included in available licensing territory?
9.2. Are rights available for all Southeast Asian countries, or only specific named countries?

## 10. Worldwide Rights

10.1. Is worldwide distribution available, or is territory inherently limited?
10.2. If worldwide rights are unavailable now, is there a path to expand territory later under the same agreement?

## 11. Territory Restrictions

11.1. Are territory rights granted per-catalog, per-title, or per-agreement?
11.2. Are there any territories explicitly excluded, and can the excluded list change during the contract term?
11.3. How is territory restriction technically enforced or verified by the provider, if at all?

## 12. Licensing Fees

12.1. Is there a licensing fee, and if so, is it flat-rate, per-title, or usage-based?
12.2. Are fees paid upfront, periodically, or contingent on performance?

## 13. Revenue Share

13.1. Is there a revenue share, and on what basis is it calculated (ad revenue, impressions, etc.)?
13.2. Who is responsible for revenue reporting and payment reconciliation?

## 14. Minimum Guarantees / Minimum Commitments

14.1. Is there a minimum guaranteed payment regardless of performance?
14.2. Is there a minimum catalog size StreamFree must license or display?

## 15. Catalog Access

15.1. Which specific titles are available for licensing, and how is that list determined or updated?
15.2. Can StreamFree select individual titles, or is access offered only as a full/bundled catalog?

## 16. Content Delivery Method

16.1. How is content technically delivered — direct media files, signed URLs, an embed player, or an API?
16.2. Is there a technical integration guide or specification available?

## 17. API Availability

17.1. Is there a public or partner API for catalog access, metadata, or delivery?
17.2. What authentication is required to use it?

## 18. Media File Delivery

18.1. If media files are delivered directly, what format(s), resolution(s), and hosting requirements apply?
18.2. Is StreamFree permitted to host the files on its own infrastructure, or must they be streamed from the provider's servers?

## 19. DRM Requirements

19.1. Is DRM required for playback, and if so, which system(s)?
19.2. What are the technical and cost implications of any required DRM?

## 20. Metadata

20.1. Is descriptive metadata (title, description, year, genre, cast, etc.) provided, and in what format?
20.2. How is metadata kept up to date?

## 21. Artwork

21.1. Is poster/backdrop artwork provided, and are its usage rights included with the video license or licensed separately?

## 22. Subtitles / Captions

22.1. Are subtitles/captions included, and in which languages?
22.2. Is StreamFree responsible for adding its own captions if none are provided?

## 23. Contract Duration

23.1. What is the length of the license term?
23.2. Is the term per-title, per-catalog, or per-agreement?

## 24. Renewal

24.1. What is the renewal process, and is it automatic or requires re-negotiation?
24.2. Can pricing/terms change at renewal?

## 25. Exclusivity

25.1. Is the agreement exclusive or non-exclusive — for StreamFree, for the provider, or both?
25.2. Can the same content be simultaneously licensed to a competing platform?

## 26. Reporting Requirements

26.1. What reporting must StreamFree provide (views, ad impressions, revenue), on what schedule, and in what format?

## 27. Takedowns

27.1. What is the process and required response time for a takedown request?
27.2. Under what circumstances can the provider demand removal of specific content?

## 28. Termination

28.1. What are the conditions under which either party may terminate the agreement?
28.2. What happens to already-served or cached content upon termination?
