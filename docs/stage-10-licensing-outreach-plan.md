# StreamFree — Stage 10: Licensing Outreach Plan

**Status:** Documentation preparation only. No provider has been contacted, no form has
been submitted, no account has been created, and no agreement exists. This document plans
outreach — it does not represent that outreach has occurred.

**Foundation:** This plan is built entirely on the factual findings already established in
`docs/content-provider-requirements.md` (Stage 9), `docs/licensing-outreach-brief.md`,
`docs/licensing-provider-questions.md`, `docs/licensing-outreach-template.md`,
`docs/licensing-response-tracker.md` (all Stage 9), and the underlying Stage 8 research.
Nothing below invents a new provider capability or upgrades an unconfirmed fact into a
confirmed one.

**Standing rule, restated:** confirming general "AVOD" or "ad-supported" rights for a
provider does **not** confirm rewarded-ad-unlock permission, web-streaming rights,
Philippines eligibility, Southeast Asia rights, worldwide rights, Google Ad Manager
compatibility, media-file delivery, or API access. Each of these must be independently
confirmed in writing before any of them is treated as true.

---

## 1. Outreach Objective

To determine — directly from each provider, in writing — whether a real licensing
relationship compatible with StreamFree's model (web-based, ad-supported, with a proposed
rewarded-ad-unlock mechanic, operated from the Philippines, targeting Southeast Asia
initially) is possible with Filmhub, Sea Star Productions, and 5W1H Studios. The objective
of this initial round is **fact-finding**, not negotiation or agreement.

---

## 2. Provider-Specific Factual Context (from Stage 8 research)

### Filmhub
- Content: narrative films, series, documentary, shorts, animation.
- **Confirmed in writing** (their own Standard Distribution Deal terms): rights granted on
  an "Ad-Supported, Subscription, and/or Transactional basis, by any and all means and
  media"; agreement is non-exclusive; territory defaults worldwide with per-title
  filmmaker opt-outs; no guarantee any title is distributed to any specific channel.
- **Not confirmed anywhere**: new/independent-platform eligibility, Philippines
  eligibility, rewarded-ad mechanic, web-specific rights, Google Ad Manager compatibility,
  DRM, delivery mechanism, pricing, revenue share, minimum commitments, contract duration.
- Contact path: no public self-serve signup; buyer page routes to a licensing-team contact
  form.

### Sea Star Productions (Sea Star Film)
- Content: short-form and vertical-first micro-drama series for mobile audiences.
- **Confirmed**: states it works with "AVOD, SVOD, VOD, and FAST platforms"; is a
  licensor (seller), not a distribution-service-for-filmmakers; territory is flexible
  ("territory-based licensing possible," broader distribution recommended).
- **Specifically not confirmed, and worth flagging as a named concern rather than a
  generic unknown**: the page's own language is consistently mobile/in-app-oriented
  ("mobile-first audiences," "in-app platforms"); neither "web" nor "website" appears
  anywhere on the page. Neither "advertising" nor "rewarded" appears anywhere either —
  only the acronym "AVOD."
- Contact path: `info@seastarfilm.com` or the website contact form.

### 5W1H Studios
- Content: vertical dramas, horizontal short dramas, feature films, TV series,
  documentaries, and more.
- **Confirmed**: licenses to "international short-drama platforms, streamers, AVOD, and
  FAST channels, and regional buyers"; names existing placements (Tubi, Pluto TV, Roku
  Channel, Samsung TV Plus); dual China/international market capability; offers
  localization services (AI dubbing, subtitle translation).
- **Not confirmed**: whether the described online submission form is the same channel a
  platform/buyer would use, or only a creator/rights-holder intake path; web-specific
  rights; rewarded-ad mechanic; Philippines/Southeast Asia specifically; pricing/revenue
  share for a platform-licensing (as opposed to creator) relationship.
- Contact path: online submission form, or `distribution@5w1hstudios.com`.

## 3. Questions About Licensing Eligibility

- Does the provider license content to new, independent streaming platforms, not only
  established partners?
- Is there a minimum size, traffic, funding, or track-record requirement?
- What is the typical timeline from inquiry to signed agreement?

## 4. Web Streaming Rights

- Does the license explicitly cover web-based (browser) streaming, as distinct from
  mobile-app or connected-TV delivery?
- If the provider's existing distribution is primarily mobile/in-app (as Sea Star's own
  language suggests), does that limit or exclude web delivery entirely?

## 5. Commercial and AVOD Rights

- Is commercial use of the licensed content explicitly permitted?
- Is ad-supported (AVOD) viewing explicitly permitted, and does the license distinguish
  between subscription, transactional, and ad-supported models?

## 6. Third-Party Advertising

- May StreamFree serve its own third-party advertising around the licensed content, or
  must a specific ad stack be used?
- Are there restrictions on ad format, frequency, or placement?

## 7. Rewarded-Ad Unlock Permission

- Does the license specifically permit a "watch a rewarded ad to unlock this title"
  access mechanic, as distinct from general pre-roll/display advertising?
- **This must be asked explicitly of every provider** — none of the three has addressed
  this mechanic in any public material found during Stage 8 research.

## 8. Google Ad Manager Compatibility

- Is there any restriction on which ad-serving technology may be used alongside the
  licensed content?
- Does the provider require disclosure or approval of the specific ad-tech stack?

## 9. Philippines, Southeast Asia, and Worldwide Territory Questions

- Is a Philippines-based operator eligible to license content under this program?
- Is Southeast Asia included in available licensing territory, and if so, which specific
  countries?
- Is worldwide distribution available, or is territory inherently limited? If limited now,
  is expansion possible later under the same agreement?
- **None of these territory questions should be assumed answered by a general "worldwide
  default" or "flexible territory" statement** — each must be confirmed for StreamFree's
  specific operating base and target markets.

## 10. Pricing, Revenue Share, and Minimum Commitments

- Is there a licensing fee (flat, per-title, or usage-based)?
- Is there a revenue share, and on what basis is it calculated?
- Is there a minimum guaranteed payment or a minimum catalog commitment?

## 11. Delivery, API, and DRM Requirements

- How is content technically delivered — direct media files, signed URLs, an embed
  player, or an API?
- Is StreamFree permitted to host media files on its own infrastructure, or must content
  stream from the provider's servers?
- Is DRM required, and if so, which system(s), and with what technical/cost implications?

## 12. Metadata, Artwork, Captions, and Subtitles

- Is descriptive metadata (title, description, year, genre, cast) provided, and in what
  format?
- Is poster/backdrop artwork included with the video license, or licensed separately?
- Are subtitles/captions included, and in which languages?

## 13. Contract, Exclusivity, Reporting, and Termination Questions

- What is the contract term length, and is it per-title, per-catalog, or per-agreement?
- What is the renewal process — automatic or renegotiated?
- Is the agreement exclusive or non-exclusive, for either party?
- What reporting (views, ad impressions, revenue) is required, on what schedule?
- What are the termination conditions, and what happens to already-served content
  afterward?
- What is the takedown-notice process and required response time?

## 16. Phase 1 and Phase 2 Outreach Sequence

**Phase 1 — this document's scope**: Filmhub, Sea Star Productions, 5W1H Studios. These
three were selected because each has at least first-party confirmation of general
AVOD/commercial rights (unlike several other Stage 8 candidates whose AVOD claims were
secondary-sourced only), and together they cover both the broadest content-type range
(Filmhub) and the strongest short-drama/vertical-content fit (Sea Star, 5W1H).

**Phase 2 — deferred, not started**: Vision Films, CatchPlay/Screenworks Asia, and AR Asia
Production, per Stage 8 research. These require more basic fact-finding before a
Phase-1-style targeted inquiry is worthwhile (e.g., confirming a platform-facing contact
path exists at all for CatchPlay and AR Asia Production, since none was found in Stage 8).
Phase 2 outreach is not planned to begin until Phase 1 responses are received and
reviewed, or until a documented contact path for a Phase 2 provider is found.

## 17. Response Evaluation Checklist

When a response is received, record it in `docs/licensing-response-tracker.md` and
evaluate against this checklist before treating anything as confirmed:

- [ ] Is the response from an official representative of the provider (not a generic/auto
      reply)?
- [ ] Does it explicitly confirm — in writing — new/independent-platform eligibility?
- [ ] Does it explicitly confirm web-streaming rights (not just mobile/app)?
- [ ] Does it explicitly confirm commercial use and AVOD rights?
- [ ] Does it explicitly confirm the rewarded-ad-unlock mechanic specifically — not just
      general advertising?
- [ ] Does it explicitly confirm Google Ad Manager (or state which ad-tech is permitted)?
- [ ] Does it explicitly confirm Philippines eligibility and Southeast Asia / worldwide
      territory (as applicable)?
- [ ] Does it disclose pricing, revenue share, and any minimum commitments?
- [ ] Does it describe delivery method, API availability, and DRM requirements?
- [ ] Does it address metadata, artwork, and subtitle/caption availability?
- [ ] Does it address contract duration, exclusivity, reporting, and termination?
- [ ] Has legal review occurred before any agreement is signed?

A response that confirms only some of the above should be tracked with the specific
unresolved items still open — never rounded up to "approved" until every relevant item for
StreamFree's model is confirmed in writing.

---

## 18. Approval Rule

**No provider is approved, cleared, or usable by StreamFree until the relevant rights and
commercial terms are confirmed in writing.** This includes, at minimum: commercial and
AVOD rights, the rewarded-ad-unlock mechanic specifically, web-streaming rights
specifically, territory (Philippines and/or the specific regions StreamFree intends to
launch in), and commercial terms (pricing, revenue share, minimum commitments). A verbal
indication of interest, a generic reply, or a partial confirmation does not constitute
approval. This rule applies identically to Filmhub, Sea Star Productions, 5W1H Studios,
and any Phase 2 provider.
