# StreamFree — Licensing Outreach Template

**Purpose:** a reusable draft message for initiating a licensing conversation with a
content provider. This is a template only — it has not been sent to anyone. Customize the
bracketed fields before use, and remove any section that doesn't apply to the specific
provider being contacted.

**Do not send without review.** Confirm accuracy of every claim about StreamFree before
using this template — it must never overstate StreamFree's current status (no content is
licensed yet; no provider is approved yet).

---

## Email / Message Template

**Subject:** Content Licensing Inquiry — StreamFree (Independent Ad-Supported Streaming Platform)

---

Hello [Provider Contact Name / Licensing Team],

My name is [Your Name], and I'm reaching out on behalf of StreamFree, an independent,
ad-supported streaming platform currently in development and based in the Philippines.

**About StreamFree:**
StreamFree is a free, ad-supported web streaming service focused on movies, independent
and short films, and short-drama/episodic content. We are building a legitimate,
ad-supported viewing experience and are looking for content partners whose rights and
terms are a good fit for that model.

We are reaching out at this stage specifically to understand whether a licensing
relationship with [Provider Name] is possible, before making any assumptions about rights,
territory, or commercial terms.

**What we'd like to learn:**

1. Does [Provider Name] license content to new, independent streaming platforms?
2. Does your licensing model support web-based (browser) streaming specifically, in
   addition to any mobile or app-based delivery you may already support?
3. Do your commercial terms permit ad-supported (AVOD) viewing, and specifically, a
   "watch a rewarded ad to unlock this title" access model?
4. What territories can be licensed, particularly the Philippines and Southeast Asia, and
   whether broader/worldwide rights are available?
5. What are your general commercial terms (fees, revenue share, minimum commitments), at
   a level of detail appropriate for an initial conversation?
6. What content delivery method do you use (direct media files, an API, an embed
   mechanism), and what are the associated technical requirements?

We're at an early, exploratory stage of this conversation and want to be transparent: we
do not currently have any content licensed from any provider, and we are having this same
initial conversation with a small number of other potential partners as we determine the
best fit.

If [Provider Name] is open to a conversation, we'd welcome the opportunity to learn more
about your licensing program and share more detail about StreamFree in return.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Title/Role]
StreamFree
[Contact Email]
[Website/Company Info, if applicable]

---

## Notes for Customization

- Replace all bracketed fields before sending.
- If the provider's own public materials already answer some questions (as documented in
  `docs/content-provider-requirements.md` and Stage 8 research notes), you may remove
  those specific numbered questions and instead reference what's already known, asking
  only for confirmation or the remaining unknowns.
- Do not state or imply that StreamFree already has traffic, users, or revenue figures
  unless those figures are true and current at the time of sending.
- Do not commit to any exclusivity, minimum guarantee, or fee in this initial message —
  this is a fact-finding inquiry, not an offer.
